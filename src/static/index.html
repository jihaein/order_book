How to read this graph

Left / green: buy orders below the midpoint. Right / red: sell orders above it. Moving away from the midpoint, each step gets taller because it adds all available quantity up to that price.

The horizontal axis is price; height is cumulative quantity, not a price forecast. The dashed gold line is the midpoint between the best bid and best ask. Blue lines mark your resting limit orders.

How matching works (rules & big-O)

Limit orders rest in price-time priority (FIFO per level). Incoming orders sweep the contra side best-first; partial fills leave remainder resting. Market orders take liquidity until filled or the book is empty (remainder expires). Book levels are O(log L) per side; each match is O(1) at the touch.
